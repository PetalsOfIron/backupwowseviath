On 2026-11-25 the Etsy logo X posts said "10 EUR" but Etsy shows EUR 12.10 (VAT included, Latvia) for both listings 4625130951 and 4625151960; user said to check the price when making a post in Twitter.

**Why:** a wrong price in a post misleads buyers.
**How to apply:** open the public listing page (not the editor) and copy the displayed price (state "incl. VAT" for Etsy); Gumroad shows the price as entered (10 EUR). See [[etsy-created-by-me]], [[gumroad-selling-plan]].
